How it is calculated

  • Gross Payment = Bank payment / 0.96
  • Principal = Balance B/Fwd + Gross Payment
  • Interest = Principal x monthly rate x Tenor
  • Gross Loan = Principal + Interest
  • EMI = Gross Loan / Tenor

Bank payment and Tenor are required on every loan row. The tenor is read from each row, so loans can have different tenors. If you already have a Principal or New Principal amount filled in, it is used as it is. Rows without a loan, such as titles or notes, are left alone.
