What should I upload?

An Excel file (.xlsx or .xls) with one row per loan and a header row. Two layouts are supported:

LayoutColumnsHow the principal is found
A. New PrincipalNew Principal (required)Read straight from the New Principal column
B. Bank paymentBank payment (required), Balance B/F, TenorPrincipal = Balance B/F + Bank payment ÷ 0.96. Cells holding a placeholder such as "XXXX" are filled in

Other columns (Name, Ministry, IPPIS NO, Payment Date, Status, Start/End date and so on) are kept untouched. The Interest / Total Interest, Gross Loan / Total debt and Monthly repayment / monthly EMI columns are filled in with the results.
