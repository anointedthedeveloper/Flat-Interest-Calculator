What should I upload?

An Excel file with one row per loan and a header row. Two layouts are supported:

LayoutColumnsHow the principal is found
A. New PrincipalNew Principal (required)Read from the New Principal column
B. Bank paymentBank payment (required), Balance B/F, TenorPrincipal = Balance B/F + Bank payment / 0.96. Placeholder cells such as "XXXX" are filled in: Gross bank payment, Principal, Total Interest, Total debt, monthly EMI

All other columns (Name, Ministry, IPPIS NO, Payment Date, Status, and so on) are kept as they are.

Loan settings

Interest = Principal x Rate x Tenure
Total = Principal + Interest
Monthly = Total / Tenure
